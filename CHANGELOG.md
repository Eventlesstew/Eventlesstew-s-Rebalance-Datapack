# Tools, Weapons and Armor
## New Additions
Before we get to the balance changes, let's cover the new content first.

### Rose Golden Gear
A new tier that serves as a formidable alternative to Netherite Armor. 
- Like the Gold before it, Rose Gold Gear is faster at combat and mining. Though it's rather fragile and doesn't deliver in single-hit damage.
- Rose Golden Ingots can be crafted using 4 Copper Ingots and 4 Golden Ingots. You'll be needing that for upgrading your Golden gear.
- The Smithing Upgrades for Rose Gold can be found at Bastion Remnants. Netherite upgrades have been moved to Nether Fortresses.

### Hyphae Tools and Hoglin Hide Armor
An odd sidegrade, but you'll find it's a welcome one with it's rather unique stat spreads.
- Hyphae tools are crafted with Crimson and Warped Planks, instead of Wooden Tools.
- Hoglin Hide can be obtained by killing Hoglins, replacing their leather drop entirely.
    - In order to evoke it's true strength for armor however, you're gonna have to smelt the raw hide into the clothing fashionable for Piglins.

### The Hammer
Tired of a good chunk of inventory being taken by your tools? The Hammer is a new tiered tool that solves that problem! 
    - It can mine just about anything, just a bit slower.
    - It's also great at combat. It delivers the dense damage output of the axe, but onto a group of mobs rather than only one.

### Daggers
When bringing parity between Bedrock and Java's combat systems, it's evident that there are people who prefer the more strategic cooldowns of Java combat, while others just like to spam their sword around. The Dagger, a new tiered weapon, provides a fine compromise for Bedrock players with it's rapid attack speed.
- The dagger has a high attack speed for rapid attacks, but has low damage and zero knockback to boot.
- Damaging enchantments however, have a short cooldown when used with the Dagger, to prevent it from being too OP.
- It's the only weapon that you can make without a Crafting Table. So it's rather great if you need to make a weapon in a pinch.

### Hand Saw
An untiered tool that replaces Silk Touch completely.
- It uses Iron Ingots and Wooden Planks, making it a cheaper method to obtain certain blocks that usually don't drop themselves.
- The blue name and lack of enchantment glint are unfortunately due to limitations surrounding Bee hives/nests.

### The Staff
A brand new, VERY WIP weapon whose secondary attack provides an area damage effect.
It can be crafted using Blaze Rods and a Ruby, which is obtainable from the Nether Fortress.

### Auto Crossbow
Also VERY WIP, a new ranged weapon that automatically fires arrows.

### Shields
5 new shield types have been added!
- Mini Shield: Made from Armadillo Scutes, a small shield that you can quickly move around with.
- Gear Shield: Quick, yet takes quite a bit of time to recharge.
- Heavy Shield: Very slow, but can tank a lot of damage.
- Cluster Shield: Large blocking angle to provide protection against attacks from different angles.
- Shell Shield: Blocks all attacks around you, at the cost of stopping you in place.

The normal shield has been rebalanced to better align with the other shields.

## Balance Changes
All tools, weapons and armor have had their stats completely revised.
- Each tier has been organised into two distinct groups. Stone, Iron, Diamond and Netherite provide larger damage output and high durability, with slower speed. Meanwhile Wooden, Copper, Gold and Rose Gold provide speed, at the cost of damage and durability.
    - Golden gear has gotten a significant buff in durability, allowing it's readjusted strength to serve as a viable alternative to Diamond. It's still rather fragile though, to keep on par with it's archetype.
    - Hyphae is kind of it's own thing entirely, but it's stat spread leans between Copper and Gold tools.
- Netherite Upgrade Smithing templates are now found at Nether Fortresses rather than Bastions.
    - This change was made to ensure a sensible place for the Rose Golden Upgrade.

### Mining
Mining speed has been buffed across the table.

The specific stones that require higher pickaxe tiers have now been organsied into the new Mining Power stat.
- Wood and Stone have a mining power of 1, allowing them to mine Coal, Copper, Iron, Lapis and Quartz
- Copper, Iron and Hyphae have a mining power of 2, allowing them to mine Gold, Diamonds, Emeralds and Redstone.
- Diamond, Netherite, Gold and Rose Gold have a mining power of 3, allowing them to mine Obsidian and Netherite.

### Weapons
To reflect the new ideas in mind for the tiered combat system, we revised all of the stats for the weapons.
- Stone and Iron have a standard attack speed, but that drops down at Diamond and Netherite in favour of higher attack damage.
- Wood and Copper have higher attack speed, which increases with Gold and Rose Gold.

- The Axe particularly has gotten buffs to damage and attack speed to keep it's raw DPS consistent with other weapons.

### Armor
Leather and Copper Armor grant a speed boost now.
- Leather Armor provides the highest speed boost out of all armor, so it's now an optimal choice if you want to go exploring.

Chainmail armor has been completely overhauled
- They now provide a fine balance between Armor, Toughness and Speed, allowing you to remain quick while taking heavy amounts of damage.

Iron armor has gotten the least changes. All it provides is armor and has no other buffs or debuffs.

Hoglin Hide Armor may not have the speed boost and durability of Leather Armor, but it is more protective and stealthy, allowing you to sneak around unnoticed.

Diamond and Netherite Armor now slow you down, but have increased Armor Toughness as a result.

Golden and Rose Golden Armor grant a speed boost and max armor, at the cost of no armor toughness and reduced stealth.

# Blocks 

## General
- Glass no longer requires a Hand Saw to obtain
- Crimson and Warped Wart Blocks have been renamed to Pileus, and now drop Fungus and Vines when broken unless sawed.
- Crimson Pileus can no longer be made from 9 Nether Wart.
- Increased drop rate of Apples from Oak and Dark Oak. Pale Oak can also drop Apples now.
- Jungle sapling drop rate is now the same as other leaves.

## Block Variants
- Stone Slabs and Stairs now drop their Cobbled variants when mined. The same applies to Smooth Sandstone and Deepslate
- Smooth Stone has been renamed to Polished Stone and can be crafted via 4 Cobblestone or Stone.
- Stone Bricks is now crafted from 4 Polished Stone blocks.

- Sandstone has been renamed to Cobbled Sandstone.
- Smooth Sandstone has been renamed to just Sandstone
- Cut Sandstone has been renamed to Polished Sandstone
- When broken, Smooth Sandstone now drops Cobbled Sandstone, just like Stone and Deepslate.
- Deserts and Badlands now generate Smooth Sandstone instead of Cobbled Sandstone

- Polished Blackstone Bricks has been renamed to Blackstone Bricks

## Recipes
- The crafting recipes for Slabs, Stairs, Fences, Walls and more have been condensed into a 2x2 grid.
- Fences now require Logs to craft.
    - Nether Brick Fences now require the Nether Brick items
- Stone, Deepslate and Smooth Sandstone can now be crafted into their Cobbled variants.

## Redstone
- Redstone Comparators now require Copper instead of Stone
- Redstone Repeaters now require Polished Stone instead of Stone
- Hoppers now require Copper instead of Iron, and no longer require a Chest
- Powered Rails now require Copper instead of Gold
- The recipes for Powered, Detector and Activator Rails have been adjusted to better match the Rail recipe.
- Redstone Lamps now require a Torch, Redstone and Glass to craft, in a similar shape to Copper Bulbs
- Copper Bulbs now require Glowstone instead of a Blaze Rod

## Workstations
- The Stonecutter has been renamed to the Sawmill, and now works on Wood, Wool, Glass and More!
- The Composter now uses 7 planks and a piece of Dirt instead of slabs
- Jukeboxes now use an Amethyst Shard instead of a Diamond

## Storage
- Shulker Boxes have been renamed to Portal Boxes and now require Obsidian and Crying Obsidian instead of Shulker Shells.
    - This makes them way cheaper to obtain and more convenient to go building or exploring prior to defeating the Ender Dragon.
- Ender Chests no longer require a Handsaw to obtain.
- Shelves now use planks instead of Stripped Logs

## Ores
- Coal, Quartz and Emerald Ore now drop multiple of their minerals instead of one.
- Deepslate Coal Ore was replaced with Withered Remains, which can be mined into Withered Chunks
- Gilded Blackstone now drops Raw Gold instead of Gold Nuggets, and no longer drops itself unless sawed off.
