Please note that not all of the changes have been listed. If I missed any, feel free to make an issue or pull request for them.

# Primary Changes
- Enchantments have been overhauled! Redundant and useless enchantments have been removed and replaced with more fun ones.
- The entire advancement tree has been reworked entirely, with 3 new categories being added! (Combat, Construction and Food)

Death is no longer as punishing. 
- All dropped items are now bunched up into one spot instead of spreading out.
- You keep the XP you collected when you die.
- Items no longer despawn after a period of time.

## Dynamic Lighting
This system allows entities to emit light, which happens with the following:
- Glow Squids, Magma Cubes, Allays and Blazes
(Plan on making this affect Creaking, Wardens, Phantoms and Endermen as well)
- Holding Lanterns
- Having the Glowing Effect (Higher amplifiers have higher light levels)

# Gear Changes
- Stone tools have been removed. Wooden tools have been buffed accordingly.
- Mining Speed and Power is now displayed on all tools.
- Added Rose Golden Tools, a fragile, yet fast sidegrade of Gold that you can find in Bastion Remnants
- Netherite Upgrades can now be found in Nether Fortresses, along with a lot of Netherite.

- The Handsaw has completely replaced Silk Touch, allowing certain blocks to become easier to obtain.

## Hammers
- Tired of a good chunk of inventory being taken by your tools? The Hammer solves that problem! It can mine just about anything, just a bit slower.

## Weapons
- Diamond and Netherite weapons now reduce attack speed while Golden and Rose Golden weapons increase it

## Daggers
- You might like this Bedrock users! The dagger has a high attack speed for rapid attacks, but has low damage and zero knockback to boot.
- Do note that damaging enchantments have a short cooldown when used with the Dagger, to prevent it from being too OP.

## The Staff
A VERY WIP weapon whose secondary attack provides an area damage effect.
It can be crafted using Blaze Rods and a Ruby, which is obtainable from the Nether Fortress.

## Auto Crossbow
Also VERY WIP, a new ranged weapon that automatically fires arrows.

## Armor
- Leather and Copper Armor grant a speed boost.
- Diamond and Netherite Armor now slow you down, but have increased Armor Toughness as a result.
- Golden and Rose Golden Armor now increase your visibility in favour of high armor and speed.

## Shields
5 new shield types have been added!
- Mini Shield: Made from Armadillo Scutes, a small shield that you can quickly move around with.
- Gear Shield: Quick, yet takes quite a bit of time to recharge.
- Heavy Shield: Very slow, but can tank a lot of damage.
- Cluster Shield: Large blocking angle to provide protection against attacks from different angles.
- Shell Shield: Blocks all attacks around you, at the cost of stopping you in place.

The normal shield has been rebalanced to better align with the other shields.

# Block Changes

## General
- Glass no longer requires a Hand Saw to obtain
- Crimson and Warped Wart Blocks have been renamed to Pileus, and now drop Fungus and Vines when broken unless sawed.
- Crimson Pileus can no longer be made from 9 Nether Wart.
- Increased drop rate of Apples from Oak and Dark Oak. Pale Oak can also drop Apples now.
- Jungle sapling drop rate is now the same as other leaves.

## Block Variants
- Stone Slabs and Stairs now drop their Cobbled variants when mined. The same applies to Smooth Sandstone and Deepslate
- Smooth Stone has been renamed to Polished Stone and can be crafted via 4 Cobblestone or Stone.
- Stone Bricks is now crafted from 4 Polished Stone blocks.

- Sandstone has been renamed to Cobbled Sandstone.
- Smooth Sandstone has been renamed to just Sandstone
- Cut Sandstone has been renamed to Polished Sandstone
- When broken, Smooth Sandstone now drops Cobbled Sandstone, just like Stone and Deepslate.
- Deserts and Badlands now generate Smooth Sandstone instead of Cobbled Sandstone

- Polished Blackstone Bricks has been renamed to Blackstone

## Recipes
- The crafting recipes for Slabs, Stairs, Fences, Walls and more have been condensed into a 2x2 grid.
- Fences now require Logs to craft.
- Stone, Deepslate and Smooth Sandstone can now be crafted into their Cobbled variants.

## Redstone
- Redstone Comparators, Hoppers and Powered Rails now require Copper instead of Stone, Iron and Gold respectively.
- Redstone Lamps now require a Lantern to craft instead of Glowstone.
- Copper Bulbs now require Glowstone instead of a Blaze Rod

## Workstations
- The Stonecutter has been renamed to the Sawmill, and now works on Wood, Wool, Glass and More!
- The Composter now uses 7 planks and a piece of Dirt instead of slabs.
- Shulker Boxes have been renamed to Portal Boxes and now require Crying Obsidian instead of Chests. This makes them way cheaper to obtain.
- Ender Chests no longer require a Handsaw to obtain.
- Shelves now use planks instead of Stripped Logs.

## Ores
- Coal, Quartz and Emerald Ore now drops multiple of their minerals instead of one.
- Deepslate Coal Ore was replaced with Withered Remains, which can be mined into Withered Chunks
- Gilded Blackstone now drops Raw Gold instead of Gold Nuggets, and no longer drops itself unless sawed off.

# Item Changes

## General Changes
- 8 Fire Charges are now crafted from 4 Blaze powder. I plan to make it so you can throw them.

- Short and Tall Grass can now be smelted into their Dry Variants.
- Tall Grass, Dry Grass and Large Fern can now be decrafted into their shorter versions, and vice versa.
- Bushes no longer require Shears to obtain, for consistency.
- Shears can now collect the tall versions of grass and fern themselves instead of 2 of their smaller counterparts.

## New Items
- Withered Chunks: Obtainable by killing Withered Skeletons, replacing their coal drop and being used to make Soul Torches.
- Breeze Powder: Crafted from a single Breeze Rod, 4 of them can be made into 8 Wind Charges.

## Food
An overhaul of food is currently planned.
- Popped Chorus Fruit is now a food item! It teleports the user 8 blocks forward and gives 8 food and 4 saturation. 

## Potions
Three new potions are in the plans to be added: Decay, Glowing and Levitation
- Lingering Potions no longer require Splash Potions to craft

# World Changes

## Trees
Many trees have been completely reworked to look a lot better.
- Oak Trees no longer have the annoying diagonally adjacent logs.
- Giant Warped Fungi now looks much more like their smaller item form, making them different from the Crimson Fungi.

## Bastions
- Only spawns in Nether Wastes, Basalt Deltas and Crimson Forests now.

## Nether Fortress
Completely Reworked and Rebuilt!

It is now the Nether's Trial Chambers, complete with new challenges, loot and secrets.
- Do note that the vaults here will require their own Fortress Keys to obtain.

Other changes:
- Only spawns in Nether Wastes, Basalt Deltas, Soul Sand Valleys and Warped Forests now.
- Spawn rate increased to be more even with Bastions.
